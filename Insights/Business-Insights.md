# Business Insights

## 1. Revenue Performance

- Overall revenue across the analyzed period was approximately ₹1.69B.
- Revenue showed a gradual increase from May to July.
- The revenue trend indicates relatively steady business growth during the analyzed period.

## 2. Hotel Category Performance

- Luxury hotels showed stronger revenue performance compared with business hotels.
- This indicates a difference in revenue contribution between the two hotel categories.
- Further analysis could be performed to understand whether the difference is driven by room rates, occupancy, booking volume, or customer segment.

## 3. Pricing – ADR

- ADR (Average Daily Rate) remained relatively stable during the analyzed period.
- This suggests that major changes in average room pricing were not the primary driver of the observed revenue movement.

## 4. Occupancy

- Overall occupancy was around 57.55% in the displayed KPI view.
- Weekday occupancy, particularly for business hotels, requires further investigation.
- Comparing weekday and weekend occupancy could help identify demand patterns.

## 5. RevPAR

- RevPAR was approximately ₹6.52K in the displayed KPI view.
- Since RevPAR combines room rate and occupancy, it provides a useful measure of overall room revenue performance.
- Changes in RevPAR should be analyzed alongside ADR and occupancy to understand the underlying drivers.

## 6. Realisation

- Realisation was approximately 70.01% in the displayed KPI view.
- The metric can be monitored alongside cancellations and booking platforms to understand how successfully bookings convert into realized revenue.

## 7. Cancellations and No-Shows

- Cancellation and no-show patterns may be contributing to lower room utilization.
- Further investigation should focus on cancellation rates by booking platform, hotel category, and day of week.
- Understanding no-show behavior could help identify opportunities to improve room utilization.

## 8. Booking Platforms

- Booking platform performance can be compared to understand where bookings are being generated.
- Direct and offline booking patterns should be examined alongside online channels to understand their contribution to overall bookings.

## 9. Areas for Further Investigation

Based on the dashboard analysis, the following areas could be explored further:

- Why is weekday occupancy lower for business hotels?
- Which booking platforms have the highest cancellation rates?
- What factors contribute to no-shows?
- How does occupancy vary between weekdays and weekends?
- What factors explain the stronger performance of luxury hotels?
- How do ADR and occupancy jointly influence RevPAR?

## Conclusion

The dashboard provides a consolidated view of hotel revenue and operational performance.

The analysis highlights revenue trends, hotel category performance, pricing,
occupancy, realization, cancellations and booking behavior.

The identified patterns can be used as starting points for deeper analysis
and further business investigation.
